Selected Research Contributions

Radial Flow & Event-by-Event Fluctuations

  • Developing an ATLAS analysis of event-by-event mean transverse-momentum fluctuations in Pb+Pb collisions, aimed at disentangling intrinsic and geometry-driven contributions across collision centrality.
  • Performed data analysis for the ATLAS measurement of differential radial flow in Pb+Pb collisions as a contributing member of the primary analysis group.

Related Project

Nuclear Structure through Heavy-Ion Collisions

  • Served as the main analyzer for multi-particle cumulant measurements in O+O and Ne+Ne collisions within the ATLAS primary analysis group.

Related Project

  • Investigated the effects of nuclear quadrupole deformation and triaxiality fluctuations on initial-state geometry using Monte Carlo simulations.

Related Project

Phenomenology & Modeling

  • Proposed the constant-offset interpretation of \(v_0(p_{\mathrm{T}})\) induced by global multiplicity fluctuations and derived the underlying analytical framework.
